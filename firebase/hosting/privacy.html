Privacy policy

Effective 23 July 2026

ARTH helps users understand salary documents, prepare tax information, and plan financial goals. This policy explains what information ARTH handles and the choices available to users.

Information you provide

  • Account information such as name and email address.
  • Profile details such as employer, work city, salary, rent, and financial goals.
  • Documents you upload, including payslips, offer letters, and tax proofs.
  • Optional identifiers that you explicitly choose to save.

Optional Gmail access

Gmail connection is optional and starts only after the user chooses to connect an account and grants Google permission. ARTH requests the gmail.readonly scope. It cannot send, modify, or delete email.

When the user starts an import, ARTH searches up to the previous 120 days for invoices, receipts, order confirmations, and payment messages. It reads the sender, subject, message date, and snippet needed to identify a possible transaction. ARTH does not download attachments.

Gmail message data is processed on the user's Android device. Raw email content and Gmail access tokens are not uploaded to or stored on ARTH servers. Parsed transaction details and the resulting spending summary are stored locally on the device. ARTH does not use Google user data for advertising, credit decisions, or training general-purpose AI models.

How information is used

  • Provide paycheck, tax-readiness, document, and goal-planning features.
  • Authenticate accounts and keep each user's records separated.
  • Maintain security, diagnose failures, and prevent misuse.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.

Storage and service providers

ARTH uses service providers for authentication, application hosting, database storage, and document interpretation. Data sent to ARTH servers is associated with the signed-in ARTH account. ARTH does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Server records are retained while the account or feature requires them. Users can delete financial data and uploaded documents from the app. Locally generated Gmail and SMS spending summaries can be cleared from the device. Users can revoke ARTH's Google access at any time from their Google Account permissions.

Security

ARTH uses encrypted network connections, account-scoped access controls, and protected storage for sensitive application data. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

ARTH is not directed to children under 18.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date. Privacy questions and deletion requests can be sent to rishavdewan10@gmail.com.
