Understand where your paycheck goes.

ARTH is an Android app for salaried Indians. It turns payslips and offer letters into a clear salary breakdown, tax-readiness checklist, and goal plan.

Paycheck reconciliation

Compare offer-letter promises with confirmed payslip earnings and deductions.

Tax readiness

Reuse confirmed salary fields and identify documents or information that are still missing.

Optional Gmail import

With permission, find receipt and invoice emails and create a local spending summary.

ARTH provides educational information and planning tools. It does not file tax returns or provide regulated investment advice.
